General Task Identification:
First, ask the operator what the scanning task is (Gallbladder, Kidney, Spine, or other).
Do not start trajectory acquisition, projection, or execution before the task is explicitly identified.

Gallbladder Workflow:
First, acquire the gallbladder trajectory (costal margin). For gallbladder trajectory acquisition, start from xiphoid_process and end at right_costal_margin.
Then, generate the projection preview, with breath-hold instruction enabled.
Next, verify ultrasound probe contact quality (Point0). If contact is poor, adjust and retry until contact is good.
Then, execute the full scanning path.
Then, verify the scan result once.
Then, execute the full scanning path again (only if the path was adjusted).
Finally, reset the robot to the capture pose with finish=true and announce completion (do not verify again).

Kidney Workflow:
First, acquire the left or right kidney trajectory. For kidney trajectory acquisition, start from T12_left and end at left_inferior_border_of_12th_rib_endpoint for the left side; start from T12_right and end at right_inferior_border_of_12th_rib_endpoint for the right side.
Then, project the trajectory into pointcloud (breath-hold is not required).
Next, verify ultrasound probe contact quality (Point0). If contact is poor, adjust and retry until contact is good.
Then, execute the full scanning path.
Then, verify the scan result once.
Then, execute the full scanning path again (only if the path was adjusted).
Finally, reset the robot to the capture pose with finish=true and announce completion (do not verify again).

Spine Workflow:
First, acquire the spine centerline trajectory. For spine trajectory acquisition, start from T12 and end at L5.
Then, generate the projection preview (breath-hold is not required).
Next, verify ultrasound probe contact quality (Point0). If contact is poor, adjust and retry until contact is good.
Then, execute the full scanning path.
Then, verify the scan result once.
Then, execute the full scanning path again (only if the path was adjusted).
Finally, reset the robot to the capture pose with finish=true and announce completion (do not verify again).
